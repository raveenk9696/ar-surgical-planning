      About this page

      In 2018-2022, in a Penn radiology lab, I built a pipeline that turns a CT scan into 3D bone and skin models for a HoloLens, then led a lab team that grew it into a web app where several people view and annotate a scan's 3D model together in AR. That app is not in this repository. This page rebuilds the idea from scratch in 2026.

      Paper: Kariyawasam et al., "Automated medical image based anatomical point cloud generation for collaborative real-time augmented reality applications", SPIE Medical Imaging 2022. doi:10.1117/12.2612993

      Code: github.com/raveenk9696/ar-surgical-planning

      CT scan: Saltz et al. (2021), Stony Brook University COVID-19 Positive Cases, The Cancer Imaging Archive, doi:10.7937/TCIA.BBAG-2923, CC BY 4.0. Changes: converted to bone and skin surfaces, skin removed from the pelvis down, simplified for the web.

      No accounts and no server of ours. Notes live in the browsers that have this link; this browser keeps them 24 hours. Anyone with the link can see and add notes. Notes are signed by this tab. You can delete your own notes while this tab stays open, for up to 24 hours. To connect two browsers, public Nostr relays carry encrypted connection data; relays and the STUN servers (Google, Cloudflare) and the other people in the room can see your IP address, because browsers connect directly. Please do not type patient data.
